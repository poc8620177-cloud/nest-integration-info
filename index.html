A personal household integration

Thermostat controls.
Connected to home.

This personal setup connects a Google Nest thermostat to a private Home Assistant installation, bringing its readings and controls into the household dashboard.

Read the privacy notice ↗

What the connection does

Authorized household users can view temperature and humidity, check thermostat status, adjust thermostat settings, and operate timed fan controls through Home Assistant.

Google's Smart Device Management API provides access to the permitted device information and commands. Google Cloud Pub/Sub delivers device updates to Home Assistant.

Who it is for

The integration is intended for the household administrator and authorized household members. The Google account holder chooses which supported devices and permissions to authorize.

This personal project is operated by the household administrator and is independent of Google and the Home Assistant project.

Questions or access requests

Contact the household administrator using the support email displayed on the Google authorization screen. The privacy notice explains data use, storage, and how to disconnect.
