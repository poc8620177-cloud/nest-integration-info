Personal household integration

Privacy notice

How this connection uses Google-authorized thermostat data in a private Home Assistant installation.

Purpose and data accessed

This personal integration connects a Nest thermostat to Home Assistant. Depending on the permissions granted, data can include device identifiers and names, home and room labels, temperature, humidity, thermostat mode, temperature settings, fan status, and fan timer information.

How the data is used

The data supports thermostat status displays, controls, household automations, and device history. Thermostat and fan commands are sent to Google when an authorized household user or configured automation requests them.

Authorization and storage

Authorization uses Google's sign-in and consent process. Home Assistant stores the authorization credentials needed to maintain the connection. Google account passwords are not provided to this integration.

Device readings and events may be retained in Home Assistant history, logs, and backups according to the household administrator's configuration. Retention and deletion are managed through the Home Assistant installation and any configured backup systems.

Services and access

Google processes device requests and events through its Nest, Smart Device Management, and Cloud Pub/Sub services. Access to the Home Assistant installation is managed by the household administrator. Any configured remote access or backup services also operate according to their respective settings and data-handling practices.

This public information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes website requests according to its privacy statement. These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising, or forms and do not connect to the thermostat.

Disconnecting and deleting data

The Google account holder can withdraw authorization through Google's Partner Connections Manager. The household administrator can remove the Nest integration from Home Assistant and manage deletion of retained history and backups separately.

Disconnecting the integration does not automatically erase previously retained history or backups.

Contact

For questions about this personal integration or requests concerning retained data, contact the household administrator using the support email displayed on the Google authorization screen.
